Workstream 1

A Paris entity that runs

Several new people join a small office at the same time. Without simple basics, every hire and every invoice takes leadership time.
What I will look at
  • How hiring, contracts and payroll work today.
  • How budget, cash and vendor payments are tracked.
  • Which daily tasks reach the leadership team.
What I will do
  • One onboarding path for every new hire.
  • A monthly rhythm: budget, cash, vendor payments.
  • Short written policies, and automation of repeated steps.
What you get

A Paris operating manual and a monthly operations report.

Indicator

Days from a signed offer to a fully operational new hire.

Workstream 2

Fast customer onboarding, with full controls

Between a signed contract and a first payment there are checks, documents and handovers. This is where speed and compliance meet.
What I will look at

  • Where files wait: missing documents, handovers.
  • The main reasons for escalations.
What I will do
  • A document checklist for each client type.
  • One owner and one visible status per onboarding.
  • An escalation path with response times.
What you get

An onboarding playbook and a tracking dashboard.

Indicator

Days from a signed contract to the first live payment.

Workstream 3

A regulatory calendar where nothing slips

The risk is not the rule itself. It is a date without a clear owner.
What I will look at
  • The full list of filings, reports and audits.
  • How deadlines are tracked today.
  • Who does what between Regulatory Operations, Compliance, Legal and this role.
What I will do
  • One shared calendar, with an owner and a backup per deadline.
  • A responsibility matrix agreed with the MLRO.
  • An audit-ready folder, filled as the work is done.
What you get

A regulatory calendar and a responsibility matrix.

Indicator

Share of deadlines ready at least one week early.

  • Change a compliance control or policy without the MLRO and Compliance.
  • Buy or replace a tool before I understand the process behind it.
  • Add a process that slows execution.

What I will need

  • Access to finance, HR and onboarding data from week 1.
  • A weekly 30-minute meeting with my manager.
  • An introduction to the MLRO, the Regulatory Operations Lead and Finance.

I have not worked in a regulated fintech. The regulatory expertise stays with your Compliance team. What I bring is operating discipline, and recent work in a regulated environment as a consultant.
